**FINDINGS**

1. **Test 3, waiver authority (Round 4).** Test 3 records its source as law and then assigns the waiver to the Owner "if the timeline requires." A constraint from an external source cannot be waived by the Owner, and a schedule pressure is not a ground the law recognises. As drafted, the definition's only constraint can be dissolved by the person who most benefits from dissolving it. Who outside the team holds authority over this requirement, and why is that person absent from the definition?

2. **Test 3, basis and evidence (Rounds 1 and 4).** Test 3 states "Source: law" without naming the instrument or explaining why advanced electronic signatures, rather than simple or qualified ones, are the level the law requires for the documents this team signs. Tests 2 and 4 require a linked source or vendor documentation. Test 3 requires neither, so the assistant can satisfy it by self-report. Who confirms that each vendor meets the requirement, and against what evidence?

3. **Section F, option set validated by explanation (Round 3).** Section F treats the set as vetted once the assistant has answered which vendors it did not include and why. That confuses an explanation with a record. The tool that generated the option set is producing a retrospective account of its own exclusions, and the definition accepts that account as validation. Nothing requires the full candidate list to be recorded before screening. Nothing requires each cut to be recorded as it is made, with its criterion and ground. Nothing labels the not-offered answer as proposed and requires verification before it enters the record.

4. **Test 5, no elimination record and no resurrection (Round 3).** Test 5 asks only for the runner-up and why it lost. It does not require the order of elimination or the grounds and information state at each cut. It does not require infeasibility (failing test 3) to be distinguished from dominance or preference. It does not require the strongest discard to be named and resurrected at confirmation. "Why it lost" has no pass or fail criterion, and any reason given will satisfy it.

5. **Salience (Round 2).** Every test describes something the finished recommendation will visibly contain: three names, prices, residency statements, a legal claim, integration documentation and a runner-up. No test targets an absence, an assumption or a tail. This definition was written by salience.

6. **Exit and migration, the least discussed element with the largest consequence (Round 2).** The stated stakes are a year of contract and a migration. No test reaches contract length, termination terms, data export, or retrieval of signed documents and their audit trails after the contract ends. Which test reaches the cost of leaving the vendor this recommendation selects?

No test covers any of these.
